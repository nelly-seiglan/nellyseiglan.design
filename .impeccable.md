# Nelly Seiglan — Portfolio (refonte)

## Design Context

### Users
Design leaders, hiring managers, founders and potential collaborators evaluating Nelly — a
Lead / Founding Product Designer (AI-native products, ex-Hexa/Kolecto/Theodo). They skim fast,
on desktop and mobile, judging taste and craft in seconds before deciding to read deeper or reach out.
The job to be done: grasp who she is, feel the quality of her work, and get in touch.

### Brand Personality
High-end, warm, considered. Editorial rather than "techy". Confident but human. Three words:
**refined · warm · intentional.** It should feel like a beautifully art-directed magazine spread
made by someone who obsesses over detail — not a dark SaaS template.

### Aesthetic Direction
- **Light + warm.** Cream / beige paper surfaces, layered in tone for depth. NOT white, NOT dark.
- **One disciplined accent:** a deep, rich dark blue (navy/indigo) — buttons and key marks only. 60-30-10.
- **Editorial landing:** clear layered sections, generous rhythm, asymmetry, big type moments.
- **Work as the hero:** an immersive, scroll-animated gallery of her projects (media reveals,
  parallax layers, large serif names) — inspired by the immersive continuous scroll of
  samuel-medvedowsky.com, transposed to a light/beige palette.
- **Case studies keep a deeper UX narrative;** the home gets the cooler animated gallery.
- **Keep the existing navigation** (NS · Home · Projects ▾ · About · Get in touch).
- **Anti-references:** the current dark "AI glow" aesthetic; generic sliders/carousels with
  arrows+dots; cyan-on-dark, purple gradients, glassmorphism-everywhere.

### Type
- Display: **Bodoni Moda** — high-contrast editorial serif, large sizes only (luxury / high-end signal).
- Body & UI: **Hanken Grotesk** — warm, clean grotesque; labels in letter-spaced small caps.
- (Reflex fonts rejected: Fraunces, Playfair, Cormorant, Newsreader, DM, Space Grotesk, Inter, etc.)

### Design Principles
1. **Paper, not screens.** Warm layered beiges create depth; ink is a warm near-black, never pure.
2. **The work carries the page.** Media is large, cinematic, and animates with intent on scroll.
3. **One accent, used rarely.** Dark blue earns attention because it is scarce.
4. **Editorial rhythm.** Vary spacing and scale; left-aligned, asymmetric; big type breathes.
5. **Motion with restraint.** One well-orchestrated reveal beats scattered micro-animations.
