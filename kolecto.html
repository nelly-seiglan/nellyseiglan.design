Kolecto

A sales module that makes financial complexity feel usable, and a design team built to scale with it.

Role
Lead Designer · Manager
Year
2024 to 2025
Platform
Web
Focus
Complex workflows
▶ play
Challenge
Make a complex finance module usable for non-experts, and scale the team.
My role
Lead Designer & Manager: complex workflows, modular systems, hiring.
Outcome
A 2-designer team hired and structured, both still in role.
Context

Powerful, and painful.

Financial software is powerful and usually painful to use. Kolecto's sales module had to carry real complexity while staying usable, and the design had to scale as the product grew.

Approach

Complexity, made everyday.

I designed a modular sales module on top of complex financial logic, built reusable systems so it could scale, and hired and structured a design team to grow with it.
