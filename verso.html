Case study 01 · AI Research

Verso

The consumer OS made to capture the best insights for your company. One place to run AI interviews, group studies, and turn what customers really think into evidence teams can trust.

Role
Founding Designer
Timeline
Since June 2025
Scope
Product · UX/UI · Design System
Platform
Mobile + Web
Challenge

Build the consumer OS for AI research, credible enough to earn trust on both sides: the participants and the insights teams.

My role

Founding Designer across the whole product: the mobile interviewer, the SaaS report platform, the chat, and the design system.

Outcome

A platform clients pay for, trusted on both sides, with an AI-native design process the team now ships with.

Context

One platform, two experiences.

Verso is B2B2C. On one side, panel participants answer a mobile AI interview. On the other, managers and insights teams explore the results on the web. Two audiences, two experiences, one product.

The craft was making each side feel right: a human interview for participants, and evidence they can trust for the teams.

Panel participants

On mobile, answering an AI-led interview. It has to feel human, guided and effortless.

Surface · mobile interviewer
Managers & insights teams

On the web platform, digging into what the studies say. They need evidence, depth, and answers they can trust.

Surface · web platform
01 · The interviewer · mobile

An AI that interviews like the best researcher.

Less about the prompt, more about what makes a good interview.

OnboardingWe explain first

We take the time to show people how it works, so no one starts the interview cold.

VoiceActive listening

The interview is spoken, and the orb reacts as you talk, so you can see you are being heard.

Follow-upsRelevant and contextual

The AI picks up on what each person just said and asks the follow-up that fits.

GuidanceReassuring throughout

A progress bar and clear cues, so people always know where they are and what is left.

02 · The report platform

An insights platform built on evidence.

The client-facing core: a platform where every claim is backed by evidence. Dense with text yet readable, with audio and video of real participants keeping the human present.

03 · Consumer OS · the chat

Ask across studies, get a sourced answer.

Chat sits at the core of the OS: ask across one or several studies and get an answer that stays clear, sourced, and as scientific as the research behind it.

Design system · AI-native process

A design system LLMs can read.

One system across mobile and web, built to be read by LLMs, so I prototype directly in production, plugged straight into Verso's stack. It changed how the team ships.
