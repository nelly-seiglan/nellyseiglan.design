Verso

An AI interviewer that runs qualitative research, structured, nuanced, and trusted with real studies.

Role
Founding Designer
Year
Since 2025
Platform
Web
Focus
Conversational & AI UX
▶ play
Challenge
Replace human-led qualitative interviews with an AI people open up to.
My role
Founding Designer: conversational UX, AI interaction model, admin tooling.
Outcome
500+ studies run by the AI interviewer.
Context

Research that doesn't scale.

Qualitative research is slow, costly, and hard to scale. Verso set out to see whether an AI could run a real interview that people genuinely open up to, while keeping the structure and nuance a researcher brings.

Approach

Designing an interview people trust.

I designed the conversational experience end to end, how the AI opens, listens, follows up and adapts, plus the admin tooling teams use to set up studies and read what comes back.

Impact
0+

Shipped to production and trusted with real, sensitive research at scale.
