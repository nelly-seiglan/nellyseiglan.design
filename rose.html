Case study 02 · AI Assistant

Rose

A smart AI sales agent that embeds on any B2B site, feels native, and turns visitors into qualified demos.

Role
Founding Designer
Timeline
Since 2025
Scope
Product · UX/UI · Design System
Platform
Widget + SaaS
Challenge

Put a smart AI sales agent on any client site, one that feels native and turns visitors into qualified demos.

My role

Founding Designer across the product: the embedded Smart Bar, the conversation experience, and the analytics platform.

Outcome

Live on B2B sites like Pennylane, PayFit and Skello, with strong response to the product design.

Context

One agent, two sides.

Rose is a conversational agent that embeds on our clients’ marketing sites. On one side, visitors chat with it to get answers and find their way. On the other, the client’s marketing and growth teams read what those conversations reveal.

The craft was making it feel native on any site for visitors, and turning every conversation into something teams can act on.

Site visitors

On a client’s marketing site, they want concrete answers fast and a nudge toward the right next step.

Surface · embedded widget
Marketing & growth teams

Our users. They want qualified leads, and the learnings hidden in what visitors ask.

Surface · analytics platform
01 · The Smart Bar

A sales agent that feels native to every site.

We started with the widget. It had to feel like smart AI, not an old-school chatbot, and sit naturally on every client site. So I designed a centered Smart Bar with a white-label system: it always takes on the site’s primary color and adjusts to the page, like a ChatGPT bar built right into the site.

White-label — Rose adopts each host site’s primary colour. Here, it wears this portfolio’s.

White-labelAlways on brand

A single variable pulls the host site’s primary color, so Rose feels native anywhere it embeds.

OpeningCentered, then aside

It opens centered to focus attention, then docks to the side so people browse and chat at once.

NavigationGuides with links

Answers can carry external links, helping visitors find the right page and information.

IntentQualifies and routes

It answers precisely, qualifies the right visitors, and points them toward a demo.

02 · The platform

Every conversation becomes a learning.

Every conversation flows into the CRM and into a dashboard I designed for our users, marketing and growth directors. It started quantitative and moved toward the qualitative: what visitors actually ask, what the site makes clear, and what it doesn’t.

It surfaces the learnings buried in a marketing site’s traffic, a source that’s precious for marketing and well beyond it.
